How to read it

· Not a smooth curve. With ~21 real candidates you get a sparse scatter, and the "Pareto front" is a short staircase through the non-dominated points — not the analytic curve in the ZDT teaching demo.
· Front marches up-right over generations. Drag the slider or Play: gen-0 seeds (baseline + first proposals) sit low-left; by gen 2–5 the fbevo/ts lineages push both fronts toward (1,1).
· IN sits above/right of OUT. That vertical gap is the generalization gap — harnesses do better on crash types seen during search than on unseen ones.
· Hypervolume (area dominated toward the origin ref point) is the single-number scorecard; watch both sparklines climb.
